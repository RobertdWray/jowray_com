# Consent, version `2026-11-v2`

Supersedes `2026-11-v1` (Rob, 2026-11-03). Still no consent checkbox: this memorial is for friends and family, and sharing a memory is the consent. The form shows one sentence under the submit button:

> By sharing, you let the family keep this memory and show it within the memorial, and do other creative, respectful things to help us remember Jo.

The checkbox "The family may contact me at this email address about this memory." is now **checked by default** (Rob, 2026-11-03); it sets `Contributor.contactOptIn` and is a preference, not consent. A contributor who leaves it checked but gives no email address has nothing to be contacted at.

`Memory.consentVersion` records `2026-11-v2` once `CONSENT_VERSION` is set to that value in Vercel (Production and Preview) and in local `.env`; memories shared before that carry `2026-11-v1`.
